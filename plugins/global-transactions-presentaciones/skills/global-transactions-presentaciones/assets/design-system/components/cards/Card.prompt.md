`Card` groups content on doc pages and slides — objectives, scope items, workstreams. `StatCard` highlights a single KPI or milestone number.

```jsx
<Card eyebrow="Objetivo" title="Conexión & Core" elevated>
  Integración directa con SPBVI Credibanco.
</Card>
<StatCard value="12" label="Semanas al Go-Live" tone="flame" />
```
